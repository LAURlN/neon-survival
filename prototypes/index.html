BUILD LAB

Five ways to replace "pick one of three spells" with actually building your character. Each demo is only the building system plus a test range, so the idea can be judged on its own. Every page has presets, a run-level stepper that grows your budget the way a run would, and a pressure control for the range.

1 · FRAME

Build the body — you think in shapes

Bolt parts onto a core. A gun shoots the way it points, from where it sits. Mass slows you, armor and blades decide what happens when something touches that side.

Easy in
Stick a gun on the front. Done.
Ceiling
Firing lines, adjacency boosts, power, mass against speed, and a hitbox you drew yourself.
Expression
Ships look and handle differently: broadside, ram, glass needle, fortress.
Risk
Facing follows movement, so on a phone joystick aiming and dodging fight each other.

2 · CIRCUIT

Route the energy — you think in flow

Reactors throw pulses across a board. Mirrors, splitters, capacitors and elements reshape them on the way to an emitter, which turns whatever arrives into an attack.

Easy in
Reactor, straight line, blaster.
Ceiling
Timing, merging, order of operations, sharing one pulse stream between several emitters.
Expression
You can watch the machine think. Boards are readable and very different from each other.
Risk
Needs board space; fiddly on a small screen unless the board stays small.

3 · KERNEL

Program the weapon — you think in sequence

A clock walks a tape of instructions. Modifiers stack onto the next action, IF and ×2 control it, and heat punishes programs that never breathe.

Easy in
One instruction is already a working weapon.
Ceiling
Conditionals, burst against sustain, heat management, cycle counting.
Expression
Programs that react: one thing up close, another at range, a finisher after a kill.
Risk
Reads as "coding"; the best line is often simply the longest modifier chain.

4 · SEQUENCER

Compose the loop — you think in rhythm

Each track is a weapon, each lit step fires it, and each weapon rewards a different rhythm. The build is the soundtrack: you hear your damage.

Easy in
Click squares, something happens on the beat.
Ceiling
A fixed note budget against four conflicting rhythm rules, plus timing bursts to enemy waves.
Expression
Highest of the five. Every player's run sounds different.
Risk
Needs sound on to shine; depth lives in the rhythm rules, which need careful design.

5 · NEST

Nest the triggers — you think in consequences

Every part has a socket that holds another part, released when its trigger happens: on hit, on kill, every half second. A weapon is a chain of "when this, then that".

Easy in
One part fires. Put a second inside it.
Ceiling
Chain reactions that only pay off if each step lands; cost against reliability.
Expression
Grenades, cluster missiles, drifting batteries, kill-fed explosions.
Risk
Runaway screens full of projectiles; hardest to balance and to keep fast on a phone.
